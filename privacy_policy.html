Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026
Developer: Fiveman Developer
Google Play Compliant
1

Introduction

Welcome to Anime Wallpaper: 4K HD Anime Girl, developed and operated by Fiveman Developer. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

2

About Our App

Anime Wallpaper is a mobile wallpaper application that provides high-quality anime artwork and wallpapers for your device. Our commitment is to deliver an exceptional personalization experience while respecting and protecting your privacy at every level.

3

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We do not collect any personally identifiable information directly from users (such as your legal name, physical address, or phone number).

Automatically Collected Information

To ensure app stability and deliver optimized wallpapers, we may process non-personal diagnostic data:

  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, and Android operating system version.
  • App Usage Statistics: Aggregate statistics on features used and anonymous interactions.
  • Storage / Media Permissions: Requested only when you choose to download or set a wallpaper on your device.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Non-personalized or personalized advertising identifiers strictly handled via official Google AdMob SDK.
  • Crash & Diagnostic Logs: Anonymous crash reports used exclusively to resolve bugs.
4

Third-Party Services & Advertising

The free version of our app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may use device identifiers and cookies to serve ads in accordance with their privacy policies.

You can review Google's privacy policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable by European law). All curated wallpapers in the app are strictly filtered for family friendliness, and we adhere to our comprehensive Child Safety Policy.

6

Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to safeguard the information processed by our application. Any transient caching of images occurs locally on your device and can be cleared at any time via Android application settings.
