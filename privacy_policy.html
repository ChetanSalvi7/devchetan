Overview

Welcome to AniWall - Anime Wallpaper, developed and operated by Ghost Developer. This Privacy Policy explains the data practices of our Android app and the third-party services it uses, including Google AdMob and a wallpaper API.

About Our App

AniWall - Anime Wallpaper provides anime artwork and wallpapers for your Android device. The app does not require login, registration, or a user account. It uses a wallpaper API to retrieve wallpapers and Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Data Collection & Sharing

Information You Provide

We do not ask for or directly collect personal information through the app, such as your name, email address, or phone number. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to send, and use it to respond to your inquiry.

Data Handled by Google AdMob

Although the app has no accounts and does not directly collect personal information, Google AdMob may collect and share the following data types, as declared in the app's Google Play Data safety section:

  • Approximate Location may be estimated from your device's IP address.
  • App Interactions include interactions with the app or ads, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics include information about app and advertising SDK performance, such as launch time, responsiveness, and energy usage.
  • Device or Other IDs include identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

AdMob may collect and share these data types for analytics, advertising or marketing, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. The data processed can depend on SDK configuration, device settings, and applicable consent choices. These data types are not necessarily anonymous.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google handles the advertising data described above under its privacy policy. You can manage your Android advertising ID through your device settings; the available controls depend on your Android version.

You can review Google's privacy policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app also uses the wallpaper API from wallpaper.cc to retrieve and display wallpapers. Requests to this service are sent to the API provider. Any data the provider processes when handling those requests is subject to its own privacy practices. Contact us using the details below for questions about the wallpaper API.

Children's Privacy

The app does not require an account or ask children to provide personal information. Third-party advertising services may process data as described in this policy. If you have a concern about a child's privacy, please contact us using the details below. You can also review our Child Safety Policy.

Data Security & Retention

Data handled by Google AdMob is subject to Google's security, retention, and deletion practices. Any data handled by the wallpaper API provider is subject to that provider's practices. The app has no user accounts, so there is no in-app account data to delete. Clearing the app's local data or uninstalling it does not by itself delete data already held by third-party services. Contact us if you have questions about data retention or deletion.
