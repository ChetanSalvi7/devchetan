Child Safety Policy

Effective Date: October 2026
App: Anime Wallpaper & Fiveman Developer Apps
Zero-Tolerance Standard
1

Introduction

At Anime Wallpaper (operated by Fiveman Developer), we are committed to providing a safe and secure environment for all users. We strictly prohibit any form of child exploitation, abuse, or harmful activities on our platform. Our policies are designed to ensure compliance with child protection laws and industry best practices.

2

Prohibited Content & Activities

We maintain an absolute zero-tolerance stance toward any activity or media that harms or endangers minors. We do not tolerate:

  • Any form of child sexual abuse, exploitation, or endangerment.
  • Sharing, promoting, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Inappropriate interactions with minors, including grooming, harassment, or solicitation.
  • Any content that promotes or normalizes child exploitation.
Enforcement Notice: Violations of these policies will result in immediate removal of content, permanent account suspension, and prompt reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and appropriate international legal authorities.
3

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter any content or activities that may pose a risk to children, please report them immediately. Users can report concerns through:

  • In-App Reporting System (using the in-app "Report Content" option if applicable)
  • Direct Dedicated Email: fiveman.developer@gmail.com
We take all reports seriously and will investigate them promptly. If necessary, we will work directly with law enforcement agencies and specialized child protection organizations to address violations swiftly.
4

Compliance with Child Protection Laws

We comply with all relevant child protection laws and statutory regulations, including:

  • The Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) (United States)
  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) special provisions for minors (European Union)
  • Other applicable local and international child safety regulations

We actively cooperate with legal authorities and organizations to ensure the highest safety standards for minors using our platform.

5

Safety Measures & Prevention

To maintain a safe and trusted environment, we implement the following measures:

  • Content Moderation: We actively monitor and remove any content that violates our child safety policies before or immediately upon surfacing.
  • User Reporting: We provide tools for users to quickly report harmful content or inappropriate behavior.
  • Education & Awareness: We encourage safe online practices and provide guidelines for responsible usage across our ecosystem.
6

Contact Us

For any questions, concerns, or emergency reporting regarding this Child Safety Policy, please reach out to us directly:
